Horizon West Theater Company is dedicated to bringing the magic of live theater to our community. Our mission is to inspire, educate, and entertain through the performing arts. By fostering a creative environment, we provide a platform for local talent to shine and offer audiences of all ages the opportunity to experience the transformative power of theater.
