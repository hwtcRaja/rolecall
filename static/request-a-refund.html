Horizon West Theater Company (HWTC) — Cancellation & Refund Policy

Horizon West Theater Company (HWTC), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Governing Authority: HWTC Board of Directors.

Section 1 — General Terms

Registration fees directly fund our nonprofit programs, instructors, and facilities. By registering, you acknowledged that fees are considered earned upon enrollment. HWTC is committed to working with families facing genuine hardship and reviews every request with care. All refund decisions are made at the sole discretion of HWTC and its Board of Directors.

Section 2 — Circumstances That May Qualify

The following circumstances may be considered for a refund, credit, or transfer at the Board of Directors' discretion:

  • Medical emergency or serious illness affecting the participant or an immediate family member (documentation may be requested)
  • Death of an immediate family member
  • Natural disaster, act of God, or government-declared emergency preventing participation
  • Program cancellation or material schedule change initiated by HWTC
  • Other extraordinary circumstances as determined by the HWTC Board of Directors

Section 3 — Circumstances That Do Not Qualify

The following circumstances are generally not eligible for refund:

  • Change of mind or personal preference
  • Scheduling conflicts that existed at or prior to the time of registration, or conflicts arising after registration due to personal choices within your control (e.g. planning travel, booking other activities, or changing personal schedules after enrollment)
  • Dissatisfaction with role assignment, casting decisions, or program content
  • Failure to attend sessions without prior written notice to HWTC
  • Removal of a participant due to behavioral or conduct concerns

Section 4 — Spot Transfer / Replacement Finder Option

If you are unable to continue in the program and you identify a qualified replacement participant, HWTC may issue a full refund upon successful enrollment of that replacement. The replacement must meet all program eligibility requirements and complete registration prior to any refund being issued. HWTC retains final approval authority over all participant transfers and replacements.

Section 5 — Alternative Resolutions & Forfeiture

In circumstances where a full cash refund is not approved, HWTC may, at its sole discretion, offer one of the following alternatives. If none of these alternatives are applicable or accepted, registration fees are forfeited in full with no further obligation by HWTC:

  • A credit toward future HWTC enrollment, valid for 12 months from the date of issuance
  • A partial refund based on the number of sessions remaining at the time of the request
  • A participant transfer to a replacement of your choosing, subject to Section 4
  • Forfeiture: If no alternative resolution is agreed upon or applicable, all registration fees are forfeited and no further compensation, credit, or refund will be issued.

Section 6 — Timing & Deadlines

Requests must be submitted no later than 14 days before the program start date. Requests submitted after this deadline will only be considered under exceptional circumstances at the Board of Directors' sole discretion. Approved cash refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.

Section 7 — Appeals

If your request is denied, you may submit a written appeal to the HWTC Board of Directors within 14 days of receiving the denial notice. The Board of Directors' decision on appeal is final.

Section 8 — Governing Authority

This policy is administered by HWTC and governed by its Board of Directors. HWTC reserves the right to amend this policy at any time. The Board of Directors has final authority over all refund, credit, and transfer decisions.

Section 9 — No Guarantee

Submission of this form does not constitute approval of a refund, credit, or transfer. HWTC's decision is final and binding. You will be notified of our decision within 5–7 business days.
