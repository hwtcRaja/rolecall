Welcome to Studio After Dark, our monthly series created exclusively for our HWTC family.

Try a song, read a monologue, share something you wrote, experiment with a character, do something completely ridiculous, or just come hang out and cheer on your fellow theater kids.

No auditions. No pressure. No expectations.
HOW IT WORKS
1Enter the lottery as a Performer, an Audience Member, or both.
2The drawing happens the Friday before the show. If you're selected, we'll email you to confirm you can still make it.
3Performer order is picked live, at random, during the event itself — so stick around all night, you never know when you'll be called up!
The studio is yours after dark.
