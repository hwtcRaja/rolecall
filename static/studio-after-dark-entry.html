Welcome to Studio After Dark, our monthly series created exclusively for our HWTC family.

Try a song, read a monologue, share something you wrote, experiment with a character, do something completely ridiculous, or just come hang out and cheer on your fellow theater kids.

No auditions. No pressure. No expectations.
Studio After Dark happens the first Saturday of every month, so if you can't make this one, there's always next month.
HOW IT WORKS
1Sign up below with the email you use with HWTC. Choose Performer (you'll perform and watch) or Audience Member (just watching).
2We hold a random drawing the Friday before the show. Everyone who signed up gets an email saying whether they got a spot.
3Got a spot? Confirm it using the link in that email, or it goes to the next person on the list.
4On the night, check in at the door with your confirmation. Performer order is picked live, at random, during the event, so stick around all night. You never know when you'll be called up!
The studio is yours after dark.
