Horizon West Theater Company (HWTC) — Ticket Policy

Horizon West Theater Company (HWTC), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Governing Authority: HWTC Board of Directors.

Section 1 — General Terms

Ticket sales directly fund our nonprofit productions. By buying tickets, you agree to this policy. All exchange and refund decisions are made at the sole discretion of HWTC and its Board of Directors.

Section 2 — Exchanges to Another Date

  • Regular tickets can be exchanged for another performance of the same production, as long as tickets are available for that date.
  • We can't guarantee the same seats, or seats in the same section. We'll offer the best available seats for the new date.
  • Exchanges must be requested before the original performance begins. Tickets for a performance that has already started can't be exchanged.
  • If the new performance has a different ticket price, any difference may need to be paid; we don't refund price differences.

Section 3 — Refunds

Refunds are not guaranteed. Every refund request is reviewed and must be approved by the HWTC Board of Directors. We encourage an exchange first where possible. Requests that may be considered include:

  • Medical emergency or serious illness affecting the ticket holder or an immediate family member (documentation may be requested)
  • Death of an immediate family member
  • Natural disaster, act of God, or government-declared emergency
  • Other extraordinary circumstances as determined by the HWTC Board of Directors

Generally not eligible: change of mind, schedule conflicts, missing or arriving late to the performance, or dissatisfaction with seats or the performance. Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method. Service fees and donations added at checkout are not refunded.

Section 4 — Rush Tickets

Rush tickets are final sale. They can never be refunded, and can never be exchanged or transferred to another performance.

Section 5 — Performances Canceled by HWTC

If HWTC cancels or reschedules a performance, ticket holders will be offered an exchange to another performance or a full refund.

Section 6 — Appeals

If a refund request is denied, you may submit a written appeal to the HWTC Board of Directors within 14 days of receiving the denial notice. The Board of Directors' decision on appeal is final.

Section 7 — Governing Authority

This policy is administered by HWTC and governed by its Board of Directors. HWTC reserves the right to amend this policy at any time. The Board of Directors has final authority over all exchange and refund decisions.

Section 8 — No Guarantee

Submission of this form does not constitute approval of an exchange or refund. HWTC's decision is final and binding. You will be notified of our decision within 5–7 business days.
